What We Do

Two kinds of work.
One practical approach.

We build AI-assisted workflows for business development and for day-to-day operations, with clear review steps so your team stays in charge.

📋

Business development

Keep opportunities, relationships and pursuits easier to manage. We build AI-assisted workflows for research, relationship tracking, proposal coordination and follow-up. CRM is part of the system, alongside the decisions and handoffs that help your team win work.

  • Opportunity researchBring planning, permit and market information into a reviewable list of potential opportunities. Your team decides which are worth pursuing.
  • Relationships and follow-upOrganize contact history, flag gaps and prepare follow-up for review. Reduce repetitive CRM updates without losing responsibility for what gets sent.
  • Pursuits and proposalsTrack deadlines, responsibilities and proposal inputs in one working process, with a person accountable for the final submission.
⚙️

Operations

Make repetitive work and handoffs easier to manage. We build workflows for recurring tasks, document handling and working knowledge, with clear review steps and ownership.

  • Recurring tasksIdentify repeated steps that can be assisted or automated, and define when a person needs to check the result.
  • Handoffs and documentationMake responsibilities and next steps explicit so work is not trapped in an inbox or one person's head.

Training and adoption support both lanes: we document each workflow, clarify ownership, and teach your team to use it.

About

Stephanie Cohen
Founder & Principal, Urban Core AI · Denver, CO & Vancouver, BC

Urban Core was founded on a simple observation after years of working inside and alongside professional services firms: most don't have a technology problem. They have a process and signal problem.

Data lives in silos. Business development is relationship-driven but poorly tracked. The tools exist — CRM, project management, marketing platforms — but they're fragmented, underused, or built without accounting for how people actually work.

I built Urban Core to close that gap — with AI and automation systems that reflect the realities of long sales cycles, complex stakeholder relationships, and the human dynamics of professional services.

No generic transformation frameworks. No dashboards that look good in a demo and go unused after 90 days. Just operational leverage — the kind that compounds.
